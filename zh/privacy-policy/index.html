我们与谁分享
SEC/003

我们与谁分享

我们与谁分享.01

One service: Resend (resend.com), the email infrastructure that delivers your message to our inbox. They act as our data processor under a standard Data Processing Agreement.

我们与谁分享.02

No other third parties receive your data. We do not sell, rent, or trade it. Your message stays in our inbox and our internal email client for as long as the conversation is active.

我们与谁分享.03

Once a project is delivered (or we agree it isn't a fit), we archive your message for up to 24 months in case you come back, then delete it from active storage. You can ask us to delete it sooner.
