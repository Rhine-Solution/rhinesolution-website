Why we use it

What we collect.01

Only what you type into the contact form: your name, your email address, your message, and the language you used (English or Dutch). Nothing else. We do not collect IP addresses, browser fingerprints, location data, device identifiers, or any analytics. We do not place cookies.

What we collect.02

To reply to you. That's it. The legal basis under GDPR Article 6(1)(b) is that processing is necessary to respond to your enquiry. We do not market to you, build profiles on you, or share your information with anyone outside the chain needed to send you our reply.
